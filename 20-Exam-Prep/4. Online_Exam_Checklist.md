# Day 20: OCI Online Proctored Exam Checklist

This document outlines the mandatory checklist that must be completed before starting an online proctored OCI certification exam.

### Before the Exam (Check-in Process)

The check-in process is divided into two main stages to ensure system compatibility and exam integrity.

#### 1. System Requirements
-   **Supported Browsers:** The exam must be taken on the latest version of **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
-   **Browser State:** The browser's cache should be cleared before starting.

#### 2. Exam Readiness Check
-   **Network:** Any active VPN connection must be disconnected.
-   **Hardware:** A functional webcam must be enabled and accessible.
-   **Software (Proctoring Extension):** A mandatory proctoring **Browser Extension** must be downloaded and installed. This software monitors the exam session via the webcam and screen sharing to ensure the integrity of the test environment.
-   **Identity Verification:** The candidate's name on a valid, government-issued photo ID must exactly match the name registered in their Oracle certification account.

Once all the above checks are successfully completed, the candidate can agree to the terms and click "Check in" to start the exam.

### After the Exam
-   After the exam is finished and submitted, it is a required step to **remove/uninstall the proctoring browser extension** to restore normal browser functionality.

# Day 20: OCI Foundations (2025) Exam Preparation Guide

This lesson provides an overview of the OCI Foundations exam format, the topics covered, and key test-taking strategies.

### Exam Details Summary
-   **Exam Name:** Oracle Cloud Infrastructure 2025 Foundations Associate
-   **Number of Questions:** 40
-   **Time Limit:** 60 Minutes
-   **Passing Score:** 65% (26 out of 40 questions)
-   **Format:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) only. There are no questions with multiple correct answers.
-   **Cost:** Free.
-   **Credential Validity:** 24 months.

### Exam Topic Breakdown
The exam questions are distributed across four main domains:

| # | Domain | Weight | Approx. # of Questions |
| :-- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Getting Started with OCI | ~15% | 6 |
| 2 | **Core OCI Services** | **~50%** | **20** |
| 3 | Security and Compliance | ~25% | 10 |
| 4 | Governance and Administration | ~10% | 4 |

### Recommended Exam Strategy
1.  **Read Carefully:** Read the entire question and all of the answer choices before selecting an answer. Pay attention to keywords like "NOT" or "BEST".
2.  **Process of Elimination:** Identify and discard the "distractors" or obviously incorrect options first. This increases your chances of selecting the correct answer from the remaining options.
3.  **Time Management:** The exam allocates approximately 90 seconds per question. If you are stuck on a question, use the "Mark for Review" feature and move on. You can return to marked questions at the end if you have time.
4.  **Answer Every Question:** There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers. It is always better to make an educated guess than to leave a question blank.
